## 🚀 About Me

Data Analyst and AI/ML Engineer with 2+ years of research and applied project experience, turning messy datasets into decisions and production-ready models. I operate across the full pipeline — from SQL/Excel/Power BI reporting to deep learning research published in IEEE and Springer venues.

- ✅ Co-author of **4 peer-reviewed papers** (IEEE ×2, Springer ×2) in forecasting, computer vision, and NLP-adjacent domains
- ✅ Built end-to-end **ML/DL pipelines** — LSTM/XGBoost forecasting, CNN & Swin Transformer classifiers
- ✅ Hands-on with **BI & reporting stacks** — Power BI, Excel, and advanced SQL analytics for business use cases
- ✅ Comfortable owning a problem from **data cleaning → modeling → dashboarding → deployment**
